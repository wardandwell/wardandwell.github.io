Ward And Well Publisher – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Ward And Well Publisher is a private tool run by the owner of the Ward And Well Etsy shop (etsy.com/shop/WardAndWell). It is used only by that owner, on their own computer, for their own Pinterest and Etsy accounts. It is not offered to, or used by, anyone else.

What the tool does

With the owner's permission, the tool connects to the owner's own Pinterest account to create Pins for Ward And Well products on the owner's own boards (image, title, description, alt text and a link to the Etsy listing), to read the owner's own boards so it can choose the right one, and to read analytics for the owner's own Pins.

Data it accesses

Only data belonging to the Ward And Well Pinterest account: its boards, its Pins and their analytics. It does not access other Pinterest users' data, and it does not collect any personal information about Pinterest users, Etsy buyers or website visitors.

Storage and security

The access token that Pinterest issues is stored only on the owner's computer and is used only to make the requests described above. No data is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Revoking access

The owner can disconnect the tool at any time from Pinterest's settings (connected apps). Deleting the tool's files on the owner's computer removes everything it stored.

Contact

Questions about this policy: send a message to the Ward And Well shop on Etsy, or email wardandwell@gmail.com.
